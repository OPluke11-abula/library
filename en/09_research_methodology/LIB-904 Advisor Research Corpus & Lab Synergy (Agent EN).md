# Advisor Research Corpus & Laboratory Synergy: CYCU ICE AI & Visual Analytics Lab

## 1. Conceptual Mental Model

A fatal mistake commonly made by undergraduate researchers is selecting an arbitrary, detached open-source topic that possesses zero synergy with their laboratory's core intellectual heritage. Scientific research is cumulative. By aligning capstone projects directly with the established research lineage of the laboratory's principal investigator, students inherit established experimental baselines, domain knowledge, specialized hardware pipelines, and high-impact publishing channels.
